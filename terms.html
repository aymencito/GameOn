Beta terms

Last updated: 14 September 2026

ONEIN is a limited beta that helps existing football games find missing players. It is not a venue operator, payment provider or match organiser.

Using ONEIN

You are responsible for the accuracy of the game or profile information you submit. Do not impersonate another person, create misleading game posts, or use another person’s contact details without permission.

Joining games

A request to join is not confirmed until the organiser accepts it. Once accepted, players and organisers are responsible for confirming practical details directly, including venue, payment, equipment and any change to kickoff time.

Attendance and behaviour

If you accept a place, make reasonable efforts to attend or tell the organiser promptly if you cannot. Treat other players, organisers and venue staff respectfully. ONEIN may restrict accounts that repeatedly misuse the beta or create safety problems.

Payments

ONEIN does not currently collect player fees. Any payment is arranged directly between players, organisers or venues. Check the amount and payment method before attending.

Safety

Sport involves inherent risks. Use your own judgement before attending a game, follow venue rules, and do not participate if circumstances feel unsafe. In an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service rather than relying on ONEIN.

Beta availability

The service may change, contain defects, or be temporarily unavailable while the beta is improved. Features may be added, removed or changed before wider launch.

Changes

These terms will be reviewed before a wider public launch and before introducing features such as in-app payments, broader location matching or additional sports.

Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 September 2026

ONEIN is currently a limited Glasgow football beta. This notice explains the information used to operate the beta.

Information we use

When you create an account or use ONEIN, we may process your email address, first name, mobile or WhatsApp number, area, playing level, game posts, join requests, and related activity needed to operate the service.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate accounts, show relevant games, let organisers manage player requests, connect accepted players with organisers, prevent duplicate or invalid requests, and maintain the reliability and security of the beta.

Who can see what

Game information posted by organisers may be visible to people using ONEIN. Player contact details are intended to be used for game coordination after a request is accepted. Please do not post sensitive personal information in game descriptions or venue fields.

Service providers

ONEIN currently relies on third-party infrastructure for hosting, authentication, database services, email delivery and web distribution. These providers process data only as needed to provide those services.

Data retention and deletion

During the beta, game and account activity may be retained so that My Play, My Games, history, abuse prevention and troubleshooting continue to work. If you want your account information reviewed or deleted, contact the ONEIN operator using the contact method through which you were invited to the beta.

Your choices

You can choose not to join or organise a game. You should only share a mobile number you are comfortable using for direct game coordination.

Beta status

This notice will be updated before any wider public launch, especially if ONEIN introduces payments, additional sports, location-based alerts or new analytics.
